blastQR is an open-source file transfer app that works completely offline. Data is transferred through sequences of QR codes displayed on a sender device and scanned by a receiver device's camera.

It's fast enough for simple everyday picture and file transfers, and it's especially useful for air-gapped systems (computers that are completely cut off from any network). Transfers can optionally be encrypted with a password to prevent interception.

<ul>
<li>Offline & Private File Sharing</li>
<li>No Internet, No Servers</li>
<li>No WiFi, No Bluetooth, No Cloud</li>
<li>True Air-Gap Communication</li>
<li>Your Data Stays Offline</li>
</ul>

blastQR is currently available for Linux, Android, iOS, macOS and Windows.
